Lab 4: Cleaning Web Data for Visualization - How Did Elon Musk's Tweet Sentiment Change Over Time?

This visualization shows the weekly average sentiment of Elon Musk's tweets from July 2022 to June 2023. Point size represents the number of tweets posted during each week.

Source: Elon Musk Tweets

Tweets collected: 5,904

Analysis

T his dataset contains 5904 Elon Musk tweets collected between July 2022 and June 2023. During cleaning, I parsed the tweet dates, removed missing or empty tweet text, converted engagement variables such as retweets and favorites to numeric types, and checked for duplicates and invalid values. For text analysis, URLs, usernames, and numbers were normalized, followed by tokenization, stop-word removal, and lemmatization. Sentiment was estimated using the CardiffNLP Twitter-RoBERTa model. For each tweet, the sentiment score was calculated as P(positive) - P(negative). The visualization shows weekly average sentiment over time, with point size representing the number of tweets posted that week. The results show generally positive sentiment across most of the period. Sentiment reached a particularly high level around late August 2022, while it became close to neutral during December 2022 and January 2023. There was also a noticeable negative dip around March 2023, followed by a recovery in April and May.

Note: Sentiment labels and scores are model-generated estimates rather than ground-truth labels. Transformer-based sentiment models may be less reliable for sarcasm, irony, slang, or context-dependent expressions.
