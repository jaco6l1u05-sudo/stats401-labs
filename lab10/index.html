Interaction Reflection

I extended my Lab 9 choropleth and cartogram by adding voice and text search, keyboard stepping, and camera gestures. The original version depended on mouse hover, which works for exploration but can be difficult when countries are small or distorted in the cartogram. Voice and text let users jump directly to a country, while keyboard arrows support quick rank-by-rank comparison. The webcam gesture mode detects left and right motion so users can browse without touching the keyboard. Because speech recognition and webcam access vary by browser and permission settings, I kept text search, buttons, and hover as fallbacks. Each input method gives immediate feedback through linked map highlighting and a status message showing the selected country's GDP rank and value.
