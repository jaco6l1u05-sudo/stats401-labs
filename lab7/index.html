Lab 7: Temporal Commercial Network

This visualization shows how commercial relationships between companies change over a 60-day period. Each node represents a company. Node color represents the company's sector, while node size represents its transaction volume on the currently selected day. Larger nodes therefore indicate higher commercial activity. Each link represents a commercial relationship between two companies. The link tooltip provides additional information about the transaction, including transaction amount, transaction type, and transaction count. Links appear and disappear as the selected day changes, allowing the temporal evolution of the network to be observed.

Visualization and Encoding Explanation

Day 1

Temporal Network Questions

1. How does overall network connectivity change across the 60 days?
Overall network connectivity fluctuates during the first 40 days, with relatively sparse periods and a changing number of active relationships. The network becomes noticeably denser during the final 20 days, when more companies are active and more relationships are visible simultaneously.

2. Which companies become more central or commercially active over time?
Fusion Electronics is highly active throughout much of the time period, while Ion Systems and Cedar Retail become increasingly central later in the 60-day period. Their larger nodes and additional connections indicate increased commercial activity.

3. Are there periods when the network consists of relatively separate clusters?
Yes. The visualization shows several periods with relatively separate clusters. This is especially noticeable in the later part of the timeline, when a larger connected group coexists with smaller groups. These groups have relatively few connections between them.

4. Which commercial relationships appear, disappear, or become stronger over time?
Several relationships appear and disappear as the network evolves. Early relationships such as c07–c08 disappear later, while relationships such as c09-c06 emerge after about Day 41. Some of these later relationships also have relatively large transaction amounts.

5. Does the network become more or less cross-regional over time?
The network becomes more cross-regional over time. During the later days, more relationships connect companies from different regions, including connections between North American companies and companies in Asia or Europe. This pattern is particularly visible after about Day 41.
